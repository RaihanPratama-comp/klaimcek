How the AI works

  • Receipt understanding (NLP heuristics): extracts merchant, date and total from free-form receipt text, including Indonesian date and currency formats.
  • Fuzzy matching: Levenshtein edit distance + token overlap link each claim to its receipt even when names are typed differently ("Indomart" ≈ "Indomaret").
  • Duplicate detection: exact fingerprints (merchant+date+amount) plus near-duplicates (same employee & amount within a day).
  • Anomaly detection: median/MAD modified z-scores flag statistical outliers; round-number and weekend signals add context.
  • Explainable risk scoring: every flag carries a plain-language reason and a 0–100 risk score. No black box, no auto-rejection.
